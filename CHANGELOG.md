# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 17 November 2026
First public release.
- Document scanner: automatic edge detection, 8-point crop with magnifier, perspective and true-shape correction.
- Modes: Document, ID card, Book, Receipt, Whiteboard, QR.
- Filters and adjustments, on-device text recognition, export to PDF/JPG/PNG with password protection.
- PDF toolkit: merge, split, extract/remove/reorder/rotate pages, compress, repair, crop, watermark, page numbers,
  add text, sign, protect/unlock, images↔PDF, text→PDF/image and more.
- Privacy & legal section in Settings.
