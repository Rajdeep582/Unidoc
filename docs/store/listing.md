## Full description
Unidoc turns your phone into a pocket scanner and a complete PDF toolkit — without accounts, ads or uploading your files.

SCAN
• Automatic edge detection that finds the page even on busy backgrounds
• Precise 8-point crop with magnifier, perspective correction and true page proportions
• Magic, B&W, Grayscale and Original filters, plus brightness, contrast and sharpness
• Document, ID card (front + back on one page), Book (two pages at once), Receipt and Whiteboard modes
• Text recognition (English and Hindi/Devanagari on-device) with copy, share and save
• QR code reader
• Export to PDF, JPG or PNG with quality presets, page size, margins and password protection

CONVERT & EDIT PDFs
• Merge, split, extract, remove, reorder and rotate pages
• Compress, crop, repair, watermark, add text, sign and password-protect PDFs
• Images to PDF (camera photos kept at original quality), PDF to images, text to PDF/image and more

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything runs on your phone — no account, no ads, no tracking
• Your documents never leave the device unless you choose to share them
• No cloud: every scan and conversion runs on your phone

Unidoc is made in Kolkata, India.
