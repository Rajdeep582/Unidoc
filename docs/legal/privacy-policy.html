Privacy Policy

Unidoc · Effective 17 November 2026

Unidoc ("the app") is developed by Rajdeep Biswas, Kolkata, West Bengal, India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your scans and files stay on your phone. We have no servers that receive your documents, we do not use accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and we never sell data. Effective 17 November 2026.

1. Information the app handles

Camera images: when you scan, the camera captures pages that you choose to keep. Images are processed (cropping, enhancement, text recognition) entirely on your device.

Files you open: PDFs, images and Office documents you pick for the converter are read from the location you select. The app only accesses files you explicitly choose.

Documents you create: scans, converted files and recognised text are stored in the app's private storage on your device, and in places you choose when exporting or sharing.

Settings: your preferences (theme, quality, languages, optional server address) are stored locally on your device.

2. What we do not collect

We do not collect, receive or store your documents, images, text, contacts, location, identifiers or usage history. The app has no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics SDK. We do not sell, rent or share personal data.

3. Permissions

Camera — used only while you are scanning, to capture documents and read QR codes. You can deny it and still import images and PDFs.

Internet / network state — used only for the optional cloud feature described below, for the "Web page to PDF" tool (which downloads the page whose address you enter directly from that website), and by Google's on-device ML Kit libraries (section 5). Everything else works offline.

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, call logs or SMS, and it reads shared storage only through the system file picker.

4. Optional cloud processing (off by default)

Some advanced conversions (for example Office documents, PDF to Word/Excel, PDF/A and OCR for some Indian scripts) can optionally use a processing server. This is switched off by default and is used only after you turn it on in Settings and confirm.

When enabled, only the files you submit to a tool marked "Cloud" are sent, over an encrypted HTTPS connection, to the server address entered in Settings. They are used solely to perform the requested conversion and are deleted from the server when the job finishes; the result is returned to your device. Nothing else is uploaded.

If you connect a server that you or someone else operates, that operator is responsible for how the server handles files. You can turn cloud processing off at any time.

5. Third-party components

Text recognition and QR/barcode reading use Google ML Kit, which runs on your device. Google's ML Kit libraries may send limited diagnostic and usage information (such as API usage, performance and device information — not your images or recognised text) to Google to maintain and improve the service, as described in Google's ML Kit terms and privacy policy: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Other libraries (for example OpenCV, PdfBox-Android, AndroidX/Jetpack) run fully on your device and do not transmit data.

6. Storage, security and retention

Your documents remain on your device until you delete them in the app, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. App data is excluded from cloud backups. Exported files are kept wherever you save them and are under your control.

Password-protected PDFs are encrypted with AES-256 on your device. Cloud traffic, when enabled, is restricted to HTTPS.

7. Children

The app is a general productivity tool and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

8. Your rights

Because we do not hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or erase on our side; you control all data on your device. If you have a question or a request under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India), the GDPR or any other applicable law, contact us at brajdeep029@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date above will change and significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
